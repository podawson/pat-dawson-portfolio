## The distributed scene

### An open call became twenty-one editions and a stage.

When studios, galleries, and venues closed, Quaranzine created a shared destination for work made in isolation. A standing global open call became a repeatable cycle of submission, curation, layout, and publication across twenty-one editions. The project then extended into a live concert series, giving the same distributed scene a performance format without requiring a physical venue. Source submissions and finished issues preserve both the working layer and the editorial result.

## What contributors authored

Contributors supplied each edition and performance; editorial curation shaped how that work became a shared scene.
