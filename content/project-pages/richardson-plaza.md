## The revision record

### Small changes accumulated into a built plaza.

Richardson Plaza ran as a recurring owner–architect/engineer task order rather than one fixed commission. Existing-condition and demolition documentation anchored the set, followed by dated revisions, electrical coordination, and contractor progress markup. A modular kit of seating, bollard caps, wave-form benches, and tree elements made repeated decisions consistent across the site. The drawing record stayed active long enough to become a plaza that opened in 2021 and remains in use.

## Who set the next revision

The owner and engineering team set priorities and timing; the drawing set carried each decision into construction.
