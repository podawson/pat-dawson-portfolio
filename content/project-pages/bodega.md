## The repeatable activation

### One identity, rebuilt on event time.

Bodega’s corner-store identity had to survive translation into a different convention or fair footprint at every stop. Each activation began with circulation, occupancy, accessibility, and venue constraints, then used axonometric studies and renderings to adapt the shelving, checkout, and signage. Plans, elevations, finish schedules, fixture specs, and 3D models closed the work for install crews at ComplexCon LA, ComplexCon Shanghai, Art Basel Miami, and beyond. The identity stayed recognizable because the delivery system could change with the venue.

## What each venue constrained

Venue rules and install windows set the buildable limits; the design system carried Bodega’s identity through them.
