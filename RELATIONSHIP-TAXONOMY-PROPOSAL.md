# Corpus relationship taxonomy — review proposal

Status: **proposed, not wired into the site**. Revision Brief v3 requires this edge list and its reasons to be reviewed before the current vocabulary is replaced.

## Proposed filter vocabulary

- **Handoff** — the work succeeds because intent, evidence, or control survives a transfer between people or disciplines.
- **Authorship** — the system creates conditions or assistance while a person or public retains consequential choice.
- **Constraint** — external limits actively determine the form, workflow, or operating envelope.
- **Inheritance** — a later project carries forward a body of material, content, or an established system logic.
- **Iteration** — a directional edge showing one project’s method being deliberately revised or generalized in another.
