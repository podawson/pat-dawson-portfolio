## The generative practice

### Thousands of forms. Only coherent ones survived.

Triply periodic minimal surfaces and Calabi–Yau manifolds gave the project a real mathematical starting point with no direct physical analogue. A trained GPT expanded the language of each form while Midjourney and a separately trained Stable Diffusion model rendered variations. Nearly 9,000 working files accumulated across named series. Most were exploratory; a series existed only when a generated form proved coherent and strange enough to keep.

## Where judgment entered

The pipeline generated variation; human judgment decided which outputs behaved like geometry instead of plausible noise.
